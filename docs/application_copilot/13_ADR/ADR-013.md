# ADR-013: Brief Is the Product; Execution Is Delivery

**Status:** ✅ Frozen
**Date:** 2026-08-02
**Supersedes:** None

## Context

Application is a decision problem, not a typing problem. Focusing engineering on the Brief (should I apply / how / what to expect) creates the durable differentiator; focusing on browser automation creates fragility and ban risk.

## Decision

The **Application Brief is the product**. Browser pre-fill is a delivery mechanism for decisions already made. Development order reflects this: PH1 normalization → PH2 Brief → PH3/PH4/PH5 (answers/session/browser).

## Alternatives Considered

- Browser-first roadmap — rejected: leads to a fragile autofill tool with no intelligence moat.
- Brief as a minor feature — rejected: loses the differentiator.

## Tradeoffs

- **Gained:** durable value, defensible positioning, natural roadmap.
- **Sacrificed:** delayed raw automation (which was never safe anyway).

## Consequences

- Every opportunity gets a Brief before apply.
- Metrics center on Brief acceptance + decision quality, not submit volume.
