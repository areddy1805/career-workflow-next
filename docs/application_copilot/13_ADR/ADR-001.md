# ADR-001: Product Thesis — Assist-First Application Copilot

**Status:** ✅ Frozen
**Date:** 2026-08-02
**Supersedes:** None
**Related:** ADR-002, ADR-013

## Context

CareerFlow v5.0 completes opportunity discovery. The next product must reduce any manual application from any source to <30 seconds while improving quality. Two mechanisms are possible: (a) automation-first (maximize autonomous submission), or (b) assist-first (maximize intelligence + pre-fill; human retains authority over submission).

## Decision

The Application Copilot is **assist-first**: it normalizes every source, produces an intelligence Brief, resolves answers from evidence, pre-fills forms, and coaches the human to a <30s submit. The human is the final authority on submission. "Automation" is a delivery mechanism, never the goal.

## Alternatives Considered

- **Automation-first** — Rejected: ATS anti-bot (Workday/Rippling) makes autonomy fragile and ToS-risky; account bans jeopardize the primary user; maintenance tax on DOM churn is unbounded.
- **Guidance-only (no browser)** — Rejected: leaves 5–20 min of typing, failing the <30s promise.
- **Agent-as-employee (submit autonomously everywhere)** — Rejected: reputation, legal, and quality risk; violates the single-user's trust model.

## Tradeoffs

- **Gained:** reliability, trust, <30s human-time, no ban risk, fully auditable.
- **Sacrificed:** unattended bulk submission (explicit non-goal v1).

## Consequences

- Browser assistant never submits without a human gesture (ADR-002).
- Autopilot is opt-in, deferred (D-006).
- Success metric is *assisted* apply time, not submission volume.
